Common questions
When does Zola Pro launch?
We're targeting April 2026. Join the waitlist and you'll be first to know — and first to get access.
What counts as a "search"?
Every time you hit "Build my day, Zola" that's one search. On the free plan you get 3 per month. On Pro, unlimited.
Do I need an account for the free plan?
Nope. Just go to getzolaapp.com and start exploring. No sign-up required for the free tier.
What cities does Zola work in?
Any city on earth. Zola pulls from Google's database of 100M+ places, so whether you're in Austin or Amsterdam, she's got you covered.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — no contracts, no fees. Cancel anytime and you keep access until the end of your billing period.
