Common questions
What's the difference between monthly and annual?
Same Pro features either way. Annual billing saves you 33% — $39.99/year vs $59.88 if you paid monthly. Both lock in the founders rate.
What counts as a "search"?
Every time you hit "Build my day, Olli" that's one search. On the free plan you get 3 per month. On Pro, unlimited.
Do I need an account for the free plan?
Nope. Just go to getolliapp.com and start exploring. No sign-up required for the free tier.
What cities does Olli work in?
Any city on earth. Olli pulls from Google's database of 100M+ places, so whether you're in Austin or Amsterdam, she's got you covered.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — no contracts, no fees. Cancel anytime and you keep access until the end of your billing period.
